🛢️ Bunker Operation & Fuel Management Center

Record bunker operations, quantities, meter readings, sampling and documents. Final quantities and compliance must be reconciled against the applicable bunker delivery documents, MFM procedures and vessel/company requirements.

🧯 Safety Equipment & LSA/FFA Inspection Center

Central register for routine checks and service tracking of safety, LSA and FFA equipment. Use approved checklists, maker instructions and statutory requirements as controlling references.

👨‍✈️ Crew & Training Management

Manage crew matrix, familiarization, training, competency records and expiry dates. Verify all competency and certification requirements against flag, STCW, company SMS and applicable vessel requirements.

🛡️ Safety Management Center

Integrated safety register for Near Miss, Hazard Observation and Incident reports. AI-style drafting is provided as a review aid; the Master/Safety Officer remains responsible for verification and final reporting.

🛠️ Defect, Breakdown & Repair Center

Record defects from discovery through repair and closing verification. Use the vessel SMS/PMS procedures and competent-person requirements for safety-critical defects.

🔧 PMS & Maintenance Center

Track equipment, planned maintenance, due dates, running hours, work orders, defects and maintenance history. Verify intervals against the maker's manual and vessel SMS/PMS.
